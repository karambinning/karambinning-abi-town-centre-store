Walking in

Number seventy-seven sits along the pedestrianised stretch of Aylesbury's High Street — the run between the Market Square and the top end of the town centre.

Walking distance from the Market Square and the Friars Square shopping centre; about ten minutes on foot from Aylesbury railway station.

Pedestrian access only along this stretch of High Street.
